Frequently Asked Questions

What is the student-teacher ratio?

We maintain a small student-teacher ratio of 8:1 to ensure personalized attention for each child.

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM, with flexible drop-off and pick-up times.

Do you provide meals?

We provide healthy snacks throughout the day. Parents need to send lunch with their children.

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow a play-based curriculum that incorporates elements from various educational philosophies.

Do you offer part-time enrollment?

Yes, we offer both full-time and part-time enrollment options to meet the needs of different families.

How do you handle allergies?

We maintain a detailed record of allergies and have protocols in place to ensure children's safety.
